# ARCHiVE brand assets

Selected identity: **Datový otisk / Data imprint** — a modular blue and black A
with the ARCHiVE wordmark (lowercase `i`).

| Asset | Size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Logo](logo.png) | 2172 × 724 | README, documentation, project introductions |
| [Icon](favicon.png) | 1254 × 1254 | PNG favicon or application icon |
| [Social preview](og.png) | 1731 × 909 | Open Graph / social sharing, approximately 1.91:1 |

These are PNG raster assets, not SVG masters. The logo and social preview use
white backgrounds. The selected favicon (variant B, round 2) uses a white
modular A on a cobalt blue background for small display sizes.

Palette: cobalt `#1459FF`, black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`.

**Tagline:** Your iPhone backup. Your data.

**Description:** Recover and export messages, photos, contacts, notes, and more
from iPhone backups. Local-first CLI with encrypted-backup support and HTML,
PDF, JSON, and CSV exports.

## Usage

```html
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" href="/assets/favicon.png">
<meta property="og:title" content="ARCHiVE — Your iPhone backup. Your data.">
<meta property="og:description" content="Recover and export your data from iPhone backups.">
<meta property="og:image" content="https://YOUR-PUBLIC-DOMAIN/assets/og.png">
<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image">
```

Replace example paths and the domain with the public asset location.
GitHub's repository social preview is a separate repository setting: including
this image in the README does not configure that setting.

## Origin

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the selected concept 03, then
refined to remove blurred edges and simplify the favicon. The design brief was:

> A bold condensed black ARCHiVE wordmark, modular A made of cobalt blue and
> black rectangles, crisp flat shapes on white. No gradients or shadows. For
> the social banner, use “Local by design.” and “Your iPhone backup. Your data.”
> with the wordmark on the left and the modular A on the right.
